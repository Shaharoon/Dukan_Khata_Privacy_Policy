Introduction Welcome to **Dukan Khata** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Haider's Developers. Dukan Khata is designed to help shopkeepers, merchants, and small businesses record digital ledger entries, manage customer credits and debits, track payments, and securely access their business records across devices. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application. --- ### 2. Information We Collect #### A. Information You Provide to Us - **Merchant Profile Information:** When you register or update your account, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, Contact Phone Number, City, and an encrypted password (or Google Account profile details if you sign in with Google). - **Customer & Ledger Records:** Business transaction records entered by you, including customer names, customer phone numbers, transaction amounts, credit/debit balances, bills, and transaction dates. - **Support Communications:** Any details or feedback you share when contacting our customer support via email or WhatsApp. #### B. Information Collected Automatically - **Device and Diagnostic Information:** Device model, operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers, and crash logs to ensure app performance and stability. - **Advertising Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (AAID) used to serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements through Google Mobile Ads. #### C. Biometric Authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock) - If you enable fingerprint or biometric quick unlock within the app, all biometric verification is processed **entirely locally on your device** by the Android Operating System (`BiometricPrompt` API). - **We do NOT collect, access, transmit, or store your biometric data on any server.** --- ### 3. How We Use Your Information We use the collected information for the following purposes: 1. **App Functionality:** To authenticate your merchant account, prevent unauthorized multi-device access, synchronize your ledger data across authorized devices, and enable backup/restore. 2. **Customer Ledger & Reminders:** To allow you to maintain records and send payment reminders via third-party messaging apps (such as WhatsApp) upon your direct action. 3. **Advertising:** To deliver relevant ads and monetize free features via Google Mobile Ads. 4. **App Improvement & Security:** To diagnose crashes, monitor server health, troubleshoot issues, and protect against fraudulent activity. --- ### 4. Third-Party Service Providers We partner with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our application. These third parties have access to specific data only to perform these tasks on our behalf: - **Google Firebase (Google LLC):** - Firebase Authentication: For secure merchant login and account verification. - Cloud Firestore: For secure cloud database storage and multi-device synchronization. - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - **Google Mobile Ads / AdMob (Google LLC):** - Serves advertisements within the application. May collect device IDs, approximate location, and ad interaction data. - [Google AdMob Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) --- ### 5. Data Security All communications between the Dukan Khata app and our backend servers (Firebase) are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
